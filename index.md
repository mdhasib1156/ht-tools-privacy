# Privacy Policy for HT Tools

**Effective Date: November 3, 2026**

**App Name:** HT Tools
**Developer:** HT Skills Hub

## 1. Introduction

HT Tools is an educational desktop application developed by HT Skills Hub to support children's learning through interactive educational tools and activities.

We respect the privacy of our users, especially children, and are committed to providing a safe learning experience.

## 2. Information Collection

HT Tools does not collect personal information from its users.

We do not request or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or other personally identifiable information.

## 3. Offline Functionality

HT Tools is designed to work completely offline.

The application does not require an internet connection for its educational features and does not transmit users' personal information or learning activities to our servers.

## 4. Children's Privacy

HT Tools is designed for educational use, including use by children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## 5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

HT Tools does not display advertisements.

The application does not use third-party advertising networks or analytics services to track users.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer users' personal information to third parties.

## 7. Data Security

Because HT Tools operates offline and does not transmit personal information to us, users' learning activities remain on their devices.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

## 9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through our GitHub repository:

https://github.com/mdhasib1156/ht-tools-privacy/issues

Please do not share sensitive personal information in public GitHub issues.
